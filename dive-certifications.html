Dive Certifications: Roatan vs Utila

The Bay Islands of Honduras are among the cheapest places in the world to get scuba certified. Utila is legendary for backpacker-priced courses (often with free accommodation), while Roatan offers smaller class sizes, calmer boat dives, and easier flight access. Prices below are for the standard PADI/SSI Open Water Diver course.

Why certify in Roatan

  • Direct international flights (RTB) — no ferry needed
  • Boat dives on the Mesoamerican Reef minutes from shore
  • Smaller groups, more personalized instruction
  • Better for families and mixed diver/non-diver groups
  • Typical Open Water price: $350–$400

Why certify in Utila

  • Among the world's cheapest certification prices
  • Free or heavily discounted accommodation with courses
  • Backpacker/social atmosphere, dive-community vibe
  • Whale shark sightings possible year-round
  • Typical Open Water price: $300–$340 (often incl. lodging)

Bottom Line

Cheapest option

  • Utila wins on pure price — courses from ~$315 often include dorm accommodation, making the true cost $100+ lower than Roatan once lodging is counted.

Best overall experience

  • Roatan edges ahead on ratings at the top end (Roatan Divers at ~5.0), easier logistics with direct flights, and calmer, more comfortable boat diving.
